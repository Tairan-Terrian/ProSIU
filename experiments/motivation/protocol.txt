Panel (a): collateral evidence removal
- Withdrawal fractions are 5%, 10%, 15%, and 20% of training double mutants.
- Generate nested position-pair requests with the main protocol's random
  ordering and group signs. The 20% request equals the released main request.
- The intended request withdraws only the selected double-mutant records.
- The diagnostic component-wide rule also removes every valid single-mutant
  anchor and remaining training combination containing an exact substitution
  present in the withdrawn records.
- Report the percentage of measured single-mutant anchors removed and the
  percentage of retained training combinations removed. Wild type is excluded
  from the single-mutant denominator. Equality of substitutions requires both
  position and substituted amino acid.
- Aggregate each metric across the three proteins within each request seed,
  then report the mean and sample SD over five seeds.

Panel (b): hidden history dependence
- Use the released 20% withdrawal request.
- Use 128 states and 64 retained candidates per state. Histories are prefixes
  of the original 16-record histories, with lengths 2, 4, 8, and 16.
- Candidate pools contain only retained training combinations and exclude all
  original history records. Both replay conditions use identical pools.
- Execute all 16 frozen programs once with the original history and once with
  withdrawn records masked out of that history. Masking affects similarity
  sums and density. Observed values, program weights, candidate pools, and
  all retained history entries are identical between the two replays.
- Enumerate all 120 pairs of program entries per state. The denominator is
  comparisons with distinct original candidate endpoints and unequal measured
  retained outcomes. A comparison changes if at least one selected candidate
  differs after history withdrawal, including newly coincident endpoints.
- Report changed comparisons as a percentage of that denominator. This
  diagnostic quantifies stale comparison identities, rather than prediction
  error or a sign reversal of the same candidate pair.
- Save supplementary counts for affected provenance, proposal changes,
  reversed outcome order, and collapsed comparisons.
- Plot each protein's mean and sample SD over all five seeds.
